\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conc}

On AI-written exams UniPlan met O1 (\pRtwoC\% of clauses, \pRtwoS\% of sentences, above keyword rules); met O2 on the confirmed conditions (no clash, every given limit kept or named), though \plViolPct\% of plannable sentences got a pick breaking a limit the parser misread and \plNoFitPct\% of all sentences had no timetable meeting every intended limit; and missed O3's criterion (\rLR\% against \rEq\% for equal weights; paired 95\% CI \rDiffEqLo{} to \rDiffEqHi). Real students are untested.
