\section{Conclusions}\label{sec:conc}

On AI-written exams UniPlan met O1 (\pRtwoC\% of clauses, \pRtwoS\% of sentences, above keyword rules); met O2 for clashes, though \plViolPct\% of plannable sentences got a pick breaking a misread limit and \plNoFitPct\% could only get the closest timetables; and did not meet O3's ranking criterion (\rLR\% against \rEq\% for equal weights; paired 95\% CI \rDiffEqLo{} to \rDiffEqHi{} points), though every reason passed its check.
